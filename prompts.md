# diablo game prompt

# first pass

I want to build a browser game. It will be multiplayer (4 players) all local using the varios d-pads (Player 1: WASD, Player 2: left/up/right/down, and then two more).

The premise of the game is that there are 10 "climbing" levels where you have to reach the next "platform" as a group. At each level's end platform there is a mini boss. At the last platform (after level 10) there is a dragon mega boss at the top of Mount Diablo.

The last dragon boss breathe poisous breath, fire or ice and has red laser eyes.

There is an evil king who is really orange, greedy, fat, old and bad. He doesn't share and he wants all the gold money and diamonds.

Each level should get increasingly more difficult. In some of the earlier levels you will fight some baby dragons and other baddies.

As you climb you will find different weapons (nunchucks, bazookas, machine guns, super stinky fart bombs, enchanted swords, laser blaster) that you can use to fight the boss at the next platform. You can only carry one weapon at a time.

Every player has their own HP 0-100, and along the climbing path hearts will drop so you can heal, and you will slowly gain HP while you are climbing.
